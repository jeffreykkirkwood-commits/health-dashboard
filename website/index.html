A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Clarity starts here.

Can I download the app today?

Health Dashboard is an iPhone prototype in development. This site introduces the experience; a public App Store release is not currently offered here.

Does it work with Apple Watch?

Apple Watch can supply data through Apple Health and HealthKit. The dashboard runs on iPhone; there is no separate Watch app.

Is this a medical or recovery score?

No. The app displays descriptive health data and comparisons with your own targets. It does not provide diagnoses, validated recovery scores, or continuous medical monitoring.

Can it read everything in Apple Health?

No. Coverage varies by metric, device, OS version, available records, and permissions. Clinical records and specialized data such as ECG waveforms require readers not included in this release.

What happens when data is missing?

Missing values remain blank. Empty results can reflect unavailable data, access settings, or the selected time period. They are not treated as zero readings or proof of denied permission.
